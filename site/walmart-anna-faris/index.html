Walmart: Anna Faris’ Day In

To launch Walmart’s new Rx Express Delivery service, we took a page out of the most iconic sick day in movie history, Ferris Bueller’s Day Off — giving Walmart’s newest delivery offering the Hollywood treatment. We tapped Anna Faris to bring the humor, connect with our audience, and give a familiar movie (and a conveniently similar name) a new spin.

The result was an ode to staying home, doing absolutely nothing, and letting Walmart handle the rest. Ferris would be proud.

And audiences were into it. The campaign outperformed historical brand benchmarks, with over 50% of viewers watching the full 60-second spot. It was also ranked the #9 most recalled ad in the US in Q3 2025 by Cubery.

And for anyone paying close attention, we snuck in some Easter eggs from the original movie. Bueller? Bueller? How many can you spot?
